## Research Projects

### RAGU (2025–2026) · NSU Research Lab
*Multi-step GraphRAG engine with compact domain-adapted LLM*
- Investigated the applicability of small language models within a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline for domain-specific tasks
- Conducted comparative evaluation of model performance using retrieval and generation metrics
- Collected and curated benchmark datasets for systematic evaluation
- **Results:** Published as [arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.11683](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11683) (2026)

### Reasoning Distillation to Small LMs (2025) · Yandex Camp
- Applied knowledge distillation techniques to compress large language models
- Improved efficiency of small models while preserving reasoning capabilities
- Experimented with prompt-based and supervised distillation approaches

### TERMinator (2023) · IIS Research Lab
*NLP pipeline for automatic scientific term extraction*
- Developed an NLP pipeline for automatic scientific term extraction
- Implemented and fine-tuned transformer-based models for sequence labeling
- **Results:** Published at [COLING Workshop (2022)](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.302.pdf) · [Software & Systems (12 citations)](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/izvlechenie-terminov-iz-tekstov-nauchnyh-statey)
